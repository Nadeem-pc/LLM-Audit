## FAQ

**Q: How does the audit actually work?**
You enter your current AI tools, plan tier, seat count, and primary use case. The engine compares your setup against current market pricing, flags redundancies, and recommends the cheapest configuration that covers the same workload. Calculations are deterministic — no AI guesswork for the math.

**Q: Do I need to connect any accounts or share API keys?**
No. You type in your spending manually. There's no OAuth, no integration, nothing to install. The tool does not store your name alongside the financial data.

**Q: Why don't the numbers match my actual bill exactly?**
The engine uses publicly available plan pricing updated as of May 2026. If you negotiated a custom enterprise deal or use a reseller, the estimates won't be exact. But most teams aren't on custom pricing — so if the numbers feel "off," you're probably overpaying without realizing it.

**Q: Can I share the report with my team?**
Yes. Every completed audit generates a public URL with a clean, standalone report page. Your team lead or CFO can view it without logging in or creating an account.

**Q: Is this affiliated with any of the AI tool vendors listed?**
No. LLM Audit is independent. Recommendations are based only on pricing data and your stated use case. No referral fees, no vendor bias.
