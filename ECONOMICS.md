# Lead Economics & Funnel Math

The business model of LLM Audit isn't charging for the audit (which is free), but generating high-intent leads for professional financial services or enterprise AI infrastructure providers (like Credex). 

## Converted Lead Value (CLV)

A "Lead" is defined as a user who completes an audit AND enters their email to receive the full report. 

- **Basic Lead:** $50 – $100 potential savings. Valuable for email lists, but lower conversion for premium services.
- **High-Intent Lead:** $500+ / month potential savings. These signify a team sizes of 15+ or heavy API usage.
- **Estimated Lead Value:** If Credex earns $500/year per client referred, and we convert 10% of high-intent leads, each high-intent audit is worth **$50 in expected revenue**.

## Conversion Funnel Assumptions

1. **Visitor to Form Interaction:** 70% (High, curiosity driven)
2. **Form Start to Completion:** 40% (Form is 3 steps, some drop-off expected)
3. **Completion to Lead Capture (Email):** 30% (Users who truly care about the results)
4. **Lead Capture to "High Intent" (>$500/mo savings):** 15%

**Funnel Calculation (Example: 1,000 Visitors):**
- 1,000 Visitors
- 700 Form Starts
- 280 Audits Completed
- 84 Emails Captured (Leads)
- 12 "High Intent" Leads (Potential Enterprise Clients)

## CAC (Cost Per Acquisition) Estimates

Given the GTM strategy (Reddit, X, Product Hunt), the initial CAC is effectively $0 + hours worked. 

If we move to **Paid Acquisition (Google Search Ads)** targeting "AI API spend optimization":
- **Estimated CPC:** $4.00 (High competition for "B2B efficiency")
- **Visit-to-Lead Rate:** 8.4% (Based on funnel above)
- **CPL (Cost Per Lead):** ~$47.00
- **CAC for High Intent:** ~$330.00

## The ROI Logic

If each high-intent lead has a lifetime value (LTV) of $1,000+ to a service provider like Credex, spending $330 to acquire them through a free audit tool is a significantly better ROI than a standard "Book a Demo" page. The tool acts as a **Lead Magnet** that qualifies the customer by their own data.

## Why High-Intent Audits are Valuable
High-intent users are often "ready to buy." They've just been shown exactly how much they are bleeding ($6k/year). They aren't looking for "information"—they are looking for a **solution**. This makes the click-through rate to a partner service (like Credex) much higher than a cold lead.
