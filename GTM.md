# Go-To-Market (GTM) Strategy: The First 100 Users

LLM Audit isn't a general utility; it's a "painkiller" for founders/CTOs who just realized their monthly AI bill hit $5k without them noticing. My GTM strategy bypasses traditional SEO (which takes too long) and focuses on high-density founder communities.

## Phase 1: High-Signal Communities (Days 1–7)

### Reddit (`r/startups`, `r/SaaS`, `r/IndieHackers`)
Instead of a "check out my app" post, I'll lead with value: *"I spent 4 hours auditing my AI stack and found $400 in waste. Here's a free tool if you want to do the same."* The goal is to solve the immediate "Am I overpaying?" fear.

### X (Twitter) Founder Circles
Targeting the #buildinpublic crowd. I'll post a screenshot of a "High Impact" audit result showing $10k+ savings. Tagging people who talk about "AI infrastructure costs" is the fastest way to get retweets within the target demographic.

## Phase 2: The Product Hunt "Wall of Savings" (Day 14)
I'll launch on Product Hunt with a focus on "The Calculator That Pays For Itself." The key hook: the shareable public report. I want people to post their "Efficiency Scores" in the comments, creating a competitive viral loop.

## The First 100 User Strategy

### 1. Manual Outreach to YC Benchmarking
There are lists of recently funded YC startups. These companies are currently scaling their AI usage and are often the most "wasteful" during the early growth phase. I will reach out to 20 CTOs personally with a "Preliminary Audit" snippet to get them onto the site.

### 2. The "Receipt" Viral Hook
I'll implement a "Share to X" button specifically for the savings headline (e.g., *"Just saved $4,200 on my AI stack using LLM Audit"*). People love to brag about being fiscally responsible while using cutting-edge tech.

### 3. Indie Hacker Milestone Posts
Post the "Revenue vs. Cost" paradox on IH. Every indie hacker is worried about their API bill. Demonstrating exactly how to switch from GPT-4 to Claude 3.5 Sonnet to save 40% is an instant click.

## Target User Profile
- **Primary:** CTOs or Engineering Leads of seed-stage startups (5–30 people).
- **Secondary:** Pro-sumer Indie Hackers running multiple model-heavy apps.
- **The "Internal Hero":** The developer who wants to show their boss they can cut costs by $2k without slowing down.

## Success Metric for GTM
1,000 unique audits run in the first 30 days, with 10% of those resulting in a "Shareable Report" being generated and posted on social media.
